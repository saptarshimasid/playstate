FIT / 01
THE FIT CHECK

The bat-fit check.

A good innings starts with a bat you can control, not just one you like the look of.

How do I choose a cricket bat?

Compare bat length, handle reach and pickup weight with your height and stance. Hold it as you would in a game and try a controlled practice swing. For junior players, choose a size they can comfortably manage now. Confirm whether the bat is intended for a leather, tennis or other practice ball and follow the supplier’s preparation guidance.

What belongs in a cricket kit?

Your checklist can include a bat, compatible balls, batting gloves, pads, wickets and a kit bag. Leather-ball play also calls for appropriate protective equipment, including a helmet and other protection suitable for the player and format. Check your coach or club’s requirements before playing.

PLAY / 02
KNOW YOUR BALL

Same game. Right ball.

Your age group, playing surface and game format are useful starting points.

Which football or basketball size?

Check the size required by your age group, school, club or league. Junior and adult groups may use different ball sizes. Choose a ball designed for your surface, and inflate it within the pressure range printed by the manufacturer.

What do I need for outdoor volleyball?

Start with a volleyball intended for your court or outdoor surface, a suitable net setup and a clear playing area. Check net dimensions, mounting needs and whether poles are included. Keep the ball clean and dry between sessions.

CARE / 03
✳READY FOR NEXT TIME

The after-play routine.

A few minutes of care can make the next session feel a whole lot better.

How should I care for sports gear?

Wipe off dirt, let damp equipment air-dry and keep it in a dry place away from prolonged direct heat. Check handles, seams, straps and strings before your next game. Follow the care instructions for each item, particularly for willow bats, leather balls and padded gear.

How do I choose hockey or golf equipment?

For field hockey, compare stick length, weight and bow shape, and check the ball recommended for your pitch. For golf, consider club length, shaft flex, loft and handedness. A coach, fitting session or knowledgeable supplier can help you narrow your options.
